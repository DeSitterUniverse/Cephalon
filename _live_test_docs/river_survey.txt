River Survey Field Guide

The North River survey measures water temperature, turbidity, and dissolved oxygen at three stations: Pine Bend, Old Mill, and Harbor Gate. Measurements are collected at 08:00 and 16:00. A turbidity value above 30 NTU requires a repeat sample and a photograph of the shoreline.

For the autumn survey, Harbor Gate recorded the highest turbidity value: 42 NTU on October 14. The team repeated the sample at 16:00 and confirmed 41 NTU. Pine Bend is not the station with the highest value.

Sample bottles should be refrigerated after collection. Field teams must not use the deployment launch journal for survey records.
